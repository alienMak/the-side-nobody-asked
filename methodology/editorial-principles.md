# Editorial principles

Each episode aims to offer a central question, a coherent narrative, an evidence-supported reversal where appropriate, and a lasting afterthought.

- Curiosity before certainty; evidence before narrative convenience.
- No manufactured twists, invented studies or unsupported claims.
- Distinguish findings from interpretation and identify illustrative scenes, reconstructions and synthetic media where relevant.
- Treat sensitive subjects respectfully; do not present entertainment as diagnosis or professional advice.
- Maintain independence from employers, universities and other research projects; no institutional endorsement is implied.
- Disclose material AI assistance accurately; do not claim independent verification where it has not occurred.
- Welcome evidence-based corrections and preserve substantive changes.
