# The Side Nobody Asked

**The story doesn't end when the video does.**

An independent science communication initiative by Mayukh Paul, exploring overlooked questions through evidence-based research and storytelling.

The aim is to investigate questions that seem familiar but become more interesting when we challenge our assumptions. Episodes combine research, accessible narratives, and evidence-supported reversals where appropriate, leaving viewers with something to think about beyond the video.

## Relationship to my research

My [Human Cyber Risk Research repository](https://github.com/alienMak/human-cyber-risk-research) explores human-centered cybersecurity, decision-making under uncertainty, trust, credibility and behavioral measurement. Some episodes may explore related ideas from a broader interdisciplinary perspective.

This channel is a separate public engagement initiative, not a source of empirical validation for VISHWAR or a substitute for peer-reviewed research. Its demonstrations are illustrative, not participant studies. The channel is independent of my employers and academic institutions; no endorsement is implied.

## Evidence and transparency

The archive distinguishes scientific findings, interpretation, uncertainty and illustrative storytelling. The records are editorial evidence reviews, not peer-reviewed publications or systematic reviews unless explicitly documented otherwise.

Evidence-based corrections are welcome.
